Streak Mechanics

PrayerMirror.sol
HOW STREAKS WORK
1 prayer per 24 hours. The contract enforces a cooldown via nextAllowedAt(address). Submit within the window and your streak increments. Miss a day (>24h gap) and the streak resets to zero. your longest streak is tracked separately and never decreases.
WHAT'S TRACKED
currentStreak — consecutive days
longestStreak — all-time best
totalPrayers — lifetime count
milestones — milestone count
prayerHash — last prayer hash
WHAT BREAKS A STREAK
Missing a day. If more than 24 hours pass since your last prayer without submitting a new one, currentStreak resets to 0. Your longestStreak and totalPrayers are unaffected — only the active streak breaks. start again from Whisper.

Governance & Voting

On-Chain Weight
⚖️

Weighted Governance

Every vote in the FOID ecosystem carries a weight computed from your prayer streak tier and MiFOID holdings. The more committed you are, the more influence you carry. No token-weighted voting — devotion is the currency.

Weight Formula

StreakVotingPower.sol
weight = (baseWeight × tierMultiplierBps / 100) + mifoidBonus
baseWeight
Fixed at 100. Same for every voter.
tierMultiplierBps
Basis points from prayer tier. Ranges from 0 (Unranked) to 500 (Foid Sovereign = 5×).
mifoidBonus
Flat +50 if voter holds ≥1 MiFOID NFT. Stacks on top of streak multiplier.

Example Weights

(100 × 0 / 100) + 0 = 0
250

(100 × 250 / 100) + 0 = 250
550
Foid Sovereign + MiFOID
(100 × 500 / 100) + 50 = 550
