## Slide 2: The Problem (30 seconds)

**Key points:**
- Wallets are just addresses — no personality, no soul
- Web3 communities have no daily ritual — no Duolingo-style retention
- Memes live on Twitter, not on-chain — culture isn't composable
- NFT traits are static — your participation doesn't evolve your identity

**Say:**
> "In Web3, we have wallets. But we don't have souls. Your address doesn't capture your contributions, your beliefs, your culture. Communities have no daily touchpoint — nothing bringing people back every day. And memes? They die on Twitter instead of becoming permanent on-chain artifacts."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (30 seconds)

**Key points:**
- Three interlocking products
- Privacy-preserving
- Built on Fluent

**Say:**
> "FOID Foundation is an on-chain altar with three products: a Prayer Terminal for daily ritual, a Loreboard Canvas for community-governed culture, and MiFOID identity NFTs that evolve with your participation. All privacy-preserving. All composable. All on Fluent."

---

## Slide 4: Prayer Terminal (40 seconds)

**Key points:**
- AI oracle generates empathetic responses
- Only hash goes on-chain — privacy first
- Streak mechanics drive retention

**Say:**
> "The Prayer Terminal lets you type how you feel — confess to Foid Mommy, our AI oracle. She responds with empathy. But here's the key: only the keccak256 hash of your prayer goes on-chain. Your raw words stay private. We track streaks — current streak, longest streak, total prayers. This creates a daily ritual habit. Your devotion becomes provable without being exposed."

**Demo tip:** If showing live, type a feeling and show the response.

---

## Slide 5: Loreboard Canvas (40 seconds)

**Key points:**
- Infinite canvas, community votes
- Epoch-based governance
- IPFS storage, deterministic settlement

**Say:**
> "The Loreboard is r/place meets on-chain governance. Anyone can propose a placement — an image, a meme. The community votes during epoch windows. Winners get permanently placed on the canvas. Everything is stored on IPFS, with deterministic settlement via a Rust VM. Culture becomes composable, governed by the community."

## Q&A Preparation

**Likely questions:**

1. **"Why Fluent?"**
   > "Fluent is building the privacy layer for Ethereum. Our prayer hashing is step one — future versions can use Fluent's native privacy primitives for fully confidential rituals."

2. **"How do you prevent spam on Loreboard?"**
   > "Base fee per cell plus optional tips. You pay for the space you take. Losing proposals get refunded minus a small fee."

3. **"What's your user acquisition strategy?"**
   > "Ritual retention — once someone starts a prayer streak, they come back daily. Plus Loreboard creates viral moments when community memes win votes."

4. **"Why would anyone pay to pray?"**
   > "Praying is free — only gas costs. The value is in the streak record and future MiFOID traits. It's proof of devotion."

5. **"What's your competitive advantage?"**
   > "We're first to combine ritual mechanics with on-chain identity and community governance. Others do one piece — we do all three, composably."

6. **"How defensible is this?"**
   > "Network effects. Your prayer history, your loreboard contributions — they're locked to your MiFOID. Switching costs increase with participation."
